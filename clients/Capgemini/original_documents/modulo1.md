## 1. Fundamentals: What is Generative AI?

**It is not magic, it is probability on a massive scale.**

* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Systems that mimic cognitive capabilities.
* **Generative AI:** A branch that creates *new* content (text, code, images) from existing data.
* **Microsoft Copilot:** Your primary AI assistant. As EMs, you are equipped with the standard MS Copilot license. This means you interact with state-of-the-art models (like GPT-5.2) through a chat interface, without the ability to build custom agents or specialized studio tools [@copilot2025].

* **Myth:** AI "knows" things.
* **Reality:** AI calculates which word has the highest probability of following, based on billions of statistical parameters and your prompts.

## EM Use Cases (Sales & Pre-Sales)

How does AI optimize the early stages of the lifecycle?

* **RFP (Request for Proposal) Analysis:** Extracting key requirements from 200-page tenders in minutes.
* **Value Proposition Drafts:** Generating the baseline for an Executive Summary highlighting Capgemini's differentiators.
* **Risk Brainstorming:** Identifying potential project risks before signing based on similar industries.

## EM Use Cases (Delivery & Operations)

The day-to-day of the EM and how to lighten the operational load:

* **Contract Management (SOWs):** Summarizing obligations, milestones, and SLAs to align the technical team.
* **Strategic Communications:** Drafting difficult emails to clients (e.g., justified delays, *Scope Creep*).
* **Executive Reporting:** Transforming raw weekly meeting notes into polished and structured *SteerCo Reports*.
* **Conflict Resolution:** Red Teaming simulations to prepare for difficult negotiations.

## Security & Privacy (The Golden Rule)

**As an Engagement Manager, you are the guardian of client data.**


| Tool Type               | Examples                      | Risk                                     | Golden Rule                                                                                                                |
| :------------------------ | :------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public (Free)**       | ChatGPT (Free), Gemini (Free) | High. Data trains the model.             | **NEVER** enter confidential data, Client Names, SOWs, or financials.                                                      |
| **Enterprise Licensed** | **MS Copilot (Your License)** | Low. Data does not train the base model. | Follow Capgemini's Cybersecurity policy. Safe for project data, but still rely on MS Learn guidelines for data governance. |

* **Note on Custom Agents:** With the standard Copilot license, you cannot create custom Copilot Studio agents. You must maximize your productivity through pure Prompt Engineering in the standard chat interface and by leveraging **MS Learn** resources.

## The Role of the "Human in the Loop"

**The final responsibility for any deliverable, code, or email generated by AI remains yours.**

* AI is an excellent assistant (A 24/7 Junior Assistant).
* AI suffers from **Hallucinations** (it invents data when it doesn't know the answer with certainty).
* **Usage Protocol:**
  1. Generate the draft with AI.
  2. Verify facts and figures by cross-referencing the original document.
  3. Add the human touch (empathy and business context).

## Exercise 3: Data "Sanitization"

**Before sending to the AI (if using non-secure tools):**

* *Original:* "The client BancoEstado owes us \$50,000 USD for Milestone 3 of the CoreBanking project."
* *Sanitized:* "The client [BANKING\_CLIENT] owes us [AMOUNT] for [MILESTONE] of the [TECH\_PROJECT]."

**Best Practices:** Create your own mental "anonymization dictionary" before copy-pasting a corporate email into the chat.
