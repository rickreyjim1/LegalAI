## 1. What is Red Teaming?

**Playing the Devil's Advocate to save your project.**

* **The Concept:** In cybersecurity, the "Red Team" attacks the system to find vulnerabilities. For an EM, Red Teaming means asking the AI to find the flaws in your project plan.
* **Why it works:** Human bias makes us overly optimistic about our own timelines. AI can objectively point out unrealistic dependencies [@redteam2026].

---

## Exercise 1: Plan Stress-Test (Red Teaming)

**Context:** You have drafted a 4-week aggressive timeline for a software rollout. You need to know where it's most likely to fail.
**Task:** Ask Copilot to attack your plan.

* **Draft Prompt to Try:**
  *"Act as a highly critical, experienced Agile Delivery Director. Review my proposed 4-week rollout plan: [Paste Timeline]. Perform a Red Team analysis. Identify the 3 most likely points of failure, resource bottlenecks, or unrealistic assumptions. Be brutally honest and suggest a mitigation tactic for each flaw."*

## 2. Managing the Delivery Team

**Constructive feedback is hard; emotional feedback is destructive.**

* As an EM, you must often deliver difficult feedback to team members who are underperforming or missing deadlines.
* MS Copilot can help you strip the emotion from your notes and reframe them into actionable, objective guidance.

---

## Exercise 2: Constructive Feedback Generation

**Context:** A senior developer has been missing daily standups and delivering code late, causing delays for the QA team. You are frustrated.
**Task:** Use Copilot to draft a professional feedback script.

* **Draft Prompt to Try:**
  *"I need to give feedback to a Senior Developer. My raw, frustrated notes are: 'He keeps skipping standups, his code is late, and QA is blocked because of him. He needs to wake up.' 
  Rewrite this as a script for a 1-on-1 meeting. Remove the emotion. Focus on the objective impact on the project timeline and the QA team. Provide a constructive opening and a clear call to action for improvement."*
